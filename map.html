Map Marker Directory

139 locations, NPCs, fishing spots, shops, wildlife and landmarks across the island.

  • 🎣 Vanya (NPC) — Your guide to fishing. Once your DG reaches Lv.2 she stops by your home with a free rod and unlocks the hobby, after which you can cast a line in any river, lake or stretch of sea on the island.
  • 🌱 Blanc (NPC) — Your gardening mentor. Talk to him at DG Lv.3 to unlock gardening. Start with potatoes — they return roughly three times what tomatoes do, the best early-game crop by value.
  • 🍳 Massimo (NPC) — The cooking mentor who works out of the café. Once you hold a Hobby Expansion Ticket at DG Lv.5, cooking is a strong first pick — it turns your catches and harvests into 2–5x the profit.
  • 🦋 Naniwa (NPC) — Your insect-catching mentor. She sells inflatable insect traps and bug nets, and is also where you can buy the 16 Dream Puzzles used to level up Dream.
  • 🔭 Bailey J (NPC) — Your birdwatching mentor. A handy leveling tip: photographing the same bird repeatedly still fills the progress bar, so you never have to chase new species across the map.
  • 🐱 Mrs. Joan (NPC) — Runs the pet adoption center. Cat care opens at DG Lv.12. Note that before you can own a dog, you must finish the basic cat-care tutorial first — it is a hard prerequisite.
  • 💰 Albert Jr. (NPC) — A wandering merchant with no fixed stall who roams the island at random. He pays more for items than the shops do, and long-pressing an item lets you sell it in bulk. Watch for him on your daily rounds.
