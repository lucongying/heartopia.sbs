🎣 Fishing Guide

How to Fish Like a Pro

Everything worth knowing before you cast a line: unlock, levels, fishing spots, weather, and the highest-value catches on the island.

🎣 Three rules to remember: ❶ fishing unlocks at DG Lv.2 via Vanya   ❷ the 91 species split across River / Lake / Sea   ❸ sell fish above 100G and cook the rest

🎣 Unlock & your first rod

Reach DG Lv.2 and the mentor Vanya visits you automatically, gifts you a fishing rod, and unlocks Fishing — your first production hobby.

📈 Fishing level 1–10

Level up by catching fish. Many rare species are gated behind higher levels — Arctic Char and Bluegill need Lv.10, Huchen needs Lv.9.

🎯 Reading the shadows

Shadow size hints at the catch: S / M / L for common fish, Golden for rare high-value species, Blue for special spawns.

Where to fish

🏞️ Rivers & ponds

Shallow River (Barbel), Tranquil River (Burbot & Salmon), Giantwood River (Huchen) and Rosy River (Carp). The Secret Pond hides the legendary Koi.

🌿 Lakes

Meadow Lake is the best beginner spot (Goldfish, Smelt, Butterfly Koi). Forest Lake (Bass, Arctic Char), Suburban Lake (Crucian Carp) and Hot Spring Mountain Lake (Bluegill) round out the set.

🌊 Sea & ocean

The Fishing Village dock is the top daily sea spot (Salmon, Sardine). Whale Sea, Zephyr Sea (Beltfish), East Sea, Old Sea and Starlight Sea (Rainbow Fish) each hold unique catches.

Weather · season · time

🌦️ Weather

Rainy and rainbow days unlock rare spawns — the Rainbow Fish appears only in rainbow weather. Clear days favor many sea fish.

🍂 Season

Tuna & Pufferfish (summer), King Crab (winter), Eel (autumn), Rainbow Fish & Rainbow Trout (spring). Most fish are year-round.

🌙 Time

Some fish only bite at night (Catfish, Eel, Legendary Koi) or at dusk; others only in daylight (Pufferfish, Rainbow Trout).

🧾 The 100G rule: sell or cook?

Sell anything above 100G straight to Albert Jr. and cook the rest — sardines (40G) become fish & chips (310G) and crucian carp (80G) becomes fried carp (350G). The one exception: a King Crab is worth far more steamed (1,100G).

🚀 Quick tips

💐 Save the rainbow bouquet

Rainbow weather already maxes rare spawns, so save your bouquet for a normal day to turn it into a mini rainbow day.

✨ Golden shadows are the jackpot

Golden shadows are extremely rare and hold the most valuable fish — wait patiently in ocean areas.

🎣 Fish near the tackle shop

Fish at the Fishing Village dock — it's next to the tackle shop for bait and repairs, and it's fishable year-round, all day.
