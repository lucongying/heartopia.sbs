🌱 Crop Guide

How to Farm Like a Pro

Everything worth knowing before you sow your first seed: unlock, farming levels, season fit, watering, and the highest-value crops on the island.

🌱 Three rules to remember: ❶ Gardening unlocks at DG Lv.3 via Blanc   ❷ rain waters your crops automatically   ❸ process cheap crops into cooking — blueberries & potatoes are worth 3–5× more

🌱 Unlock & your first seeds

Reach DG Lv.3 and mentor Blanc (near the town center) unlocks Gardening. Buy potato seeds first — the best beginner crop, returning 3× the profit of tomatoes.

📈 Reading growth time

Potatoes ripen in 2 hours (rotate several times a day), blueberries in 6 (plant before bed), wheat in 4. Time your sowings so crops are ready the moment you wake.

🎯 The priority column

The database marks the best picks — Highest (Wheat, Black Truffle), High (Potato, Blueberry…), Normal and Low. Plant high-priority crops for profit, but keep a couple of cheap types as quest reserves.

What to plant

🌾 Grains

Wheat (4h) is the backbone — almost every high-profit recipe needs it, so mass-plant from DG Lv.5. Corn (40G) and soybean (28G) are strong summer earners.

🥔 Vegetables

Potato is the king of early money (35G, 2h, all year). Tomato and lettuce are cheap quest fillers; pumpkin (55G) is the autumn money crop.

🍓 Fruit & forage

Blueberry → jam is the classic profit loop (4 berries → 170G+). Watermelon (80G) and pineapple (120G) are high-value summer harvests; black truffle (200G) is a rare autumn forage.

Season · watering · harvest

🍂 Season

Potato, wheat, carrot, onion, cabbage and lemon grow year-round. Most others are seasonal — rice and cucumber in spring/summer, corn and eggplant in summer/autumn, pumpkin only in autumn.

🌧️ Watering & rain

Rain waters your crops automatically — skip manual watering on rainy days. Check the forecast before bed: a rainy night means watered, ready-to-harvest plots in the morning.

🧺 Harvest type

Single-harvest crops (potato, wheat) need replanting each time. Multi-harvest crops (tomato, blueberry, grape, cucumber) keep producing — plant once and reap repeatedly.

🧾 Sell or process? The seed math

Process cheap crops and sell the rest. 4 blueberries (64G) become jam worth 170–320G, and a potato + fish becomes fish & chips (310G). Strawberries and grapes are the exception — selling them raw beats jam until your Cooking level is high.

🚀 Quick tips

🌙 Plant before bed

Blueberries (6h) and wheat (4h) are perfect overnight crops — sow them before logging off and harvest the moment you wake.

🌱 Keep 2–3 crop types growing

Villager quests often ask for a specific crop. Grow potatoes as your money-maker plus tomatoes/carrots as quest reserves so you never get stuck.

🚫 Skip strawberries & grapes early

Selling them raw beats making jam until Cooking is higher — save those plots for potatoes, wheat and blueberries instead.
