📈 Leveling Tips for Each Hobby

🎣 Fishing Leveling

• Prioritize high-value spots: ocean (summer) > river > pond
• Sea-fishing event week is the best time to power-level
• Match the spot to the weather: sunny = sea fishing, rainy = river fishing
• Higher fishing level → higher star-fish rate → higher sale price

🌱 Gardening Leveling

• Rotate fast crops (potato 2h) → frequent harvests = frequent XP
• Never leave fields empty — replant as soon as you harvest
• High-quality flowers (rose, lily) add extra home score
• Gardening level affects crop star rating and yield

🍳 Cooking Leveling

• Four-stove cooking: arrange 4 stoves in a square, stand in the middle to manage 4 dishes at once
• Batch-cook the same dish for max efficiency
• Prioritize high-value recipes: blueberry jam, fish & chips, fried carp
• Cooking level = higher star rate ↑ = higher price ↑ = more money ↑

🦋 Bug Catching Leveling

• Use inflatable lures to farm bugs in one spot (place in bug-dense areas)
• Different seasons/weather bring different bugs
• High-star specimens count toward home score
• The bug shop sells the puzzle dream (used to level Dream after Lv.16)

🔭 Birdwatching Leveling

• Photographing the same bird repeatedly still builds progress — no need to chase new species
• Find a fixed bird spot and level in peace
• Bird activity varies by time (most species at dawn and dusk)
• Special weather (rainbow days) sharply boosts rare-bird spawn rate

🐱🐕 Pet Leveling

• Daily interaction (feeding, petting, playing) builds affection
• Finish the basic cat-care tutorial before you can unlock dogs
• Cats and dogs can coexist — the two pets' affection is tracked independently
• Higher affection → new interactions → home-score bonus

🎒 Backpack & Warehouse Expansion · Complete Guide

Backpack space is the most underrated resource in the game — not enough slots = frequent trips home while gathering = your money efficiency is cut in half. Expansion has both free and paid paths; mixing them well maximizes value.

📊 Total free expansion: 5 × 50 slots = +250 warehouse slots. None of this needs any action — just reach the corresponding DG level. Combined with the initial capacity, pure free play reaches about 300-350 warehouse slots.

⭐ Wishing Star Expansion (Paid)

Find NPC Ka Ching (across from plot 8 in the northwest suburbs) to buy expansion with Wishing Stars. Note: backpack and warehouse expansion are bought separately, with their discount counts tracked independently.

🎯 Optimal strategy: the first 5 backpack and first 5 warehouse expansions each cost only 10 stars. Buying both to 5 times each = 100 Wishing Stars total = +50 backpack slots + 250 warehouse slots. This is the single best Wishing Star spend in the whole game — bar none. From the 6th time it jumps to 25 stars, so stop there.
💰 Wishing Star budget plan: in the beginner phase (DG 1-7) save stars for the expansion discounts → first 5 backpack (50 stars) + first 5 warehouse (50 stars) = 100 stars → after buying out, save the rest for hobby tickets → at DG Lv.12+ if you're flush, consider the 6-10th expansions (25 stars each).

Homestead System

🏠 House Building System · In Depth

Heartopia's building system has two stages: basic blueprint mode (DG Lv.4) and advanced building mode (DG Lv.15). The capability gap is huge — knowing the difference saves you from wasting time in the inefficient mode.

🔷 Blueprint Mode vs Advanced Building

Feature 🔷 Blueprint (DG Lv.4) 🔶 Advanced (DG Lv.15)
Unlock level DG Lv.4 (automatic) DG Lv.15 (automatic)
Placing walls One at a time — a single block per placement ✅ Batch placement — drag to place a whole row of walls at once
Laying floors One tile at a time, slow ✅ Batch laying — select a large floor area at once
Roof design Fixed size, not adjustable ✅ Free size adjustment — lengthen or narrow as you like
Multi-roof merging ❌ Not supported ✅ Merge multiple roofs to avoid a flat look and build depth
Furniture placement One by one, manual adjustment ✅ Batch operations + precise angle rotation
Undo/redo Limited ✅ Full undo/redo — no fear of breaking things
Use cases Placing basic functional furniture (bed, stove, storage box) Full decorating, contest-grade homes, batch construction

🏗️ DG 4-14: Using Blueprint Mode Correctly

Before you unlock advanced building (DG 4-14), don't sink lots of time into decorating. Blueprint mode is inefficient — walls that take you 2 hours now can be done in 10 minutes with advanced mode at Lv.15, and look better too.

In blueprint mode, only do this:
① Place functional furniture: bed, stoves (4 in a square), storage box, workbench
② Keep the house open — leave room for your future design
③ You can sketch floor plans (on paper or annotated screenshots), but don't build
④ Gather inspiration: visit friends' homes, screenshot layouts you like

Core principle: in blueprint mode, put all your gold and energy into production tools (seeds, stoves, expansion), not pure decoration. Only start the decorating plan in earnest after Lv.15 advanced building unlocks.

🎨 DG 15+: Decorating with Advanced Building

Once advanced building unlocks, you can finally do real interior design efficiently. Suggested order:

Advanced decorating flow:
① Floor plan first: decide each room's function (kitchen zone, bedroom zone, collection display, pet zone)
② Walls and floors: batch-lay floors → batch-build walls to separate rooms → resize roofs
③ Multi-roof design: use different heights/angles per area to avoid the "one long box" flat look
④ Furniture layout: place furniture by function, using advanced mode's precise angle rotation
⑤ Finishing touches: wall art, ornaments, plants, lighting — these details define quality

Core principle: advanced building's undo is thorough — experiment boldly with layouts, undo what you don't like. Visit friends often and screenshot ideas.

💡 Advanced House Building Tips

🏗️ Open vs Compartmented

Open plan (few/no walls): strong sense of space, great for large collections, open sightlines. Good for beginners — fewer walls = fewer mistakes.
Compartmented (many rooms): clear function zones, more privacy, great for themed design (kitchen, study, sun room). Needs advanced building's batch wall ability.

📐 Roof Design Tips

① Multi-roof merging: different heights/slopes per area → breaks the "box" feel
② Roof color matching: coordinate with the wall color — dark roof + light walls = a classic combo
③ Skylight effect: leave parts of the roof open → natural light pours in → a big sense of space

⭐ Boosting Your Home Score

Home score = furniture quality + rare collectibles + high-grade flowers
• Display 5-star collectibles (insect specimens, rare fish specimens)
• High-grade flowers: rose, lily > basic flowers
• Sculptures and special furniture score far higher than ordinary furniture
• 2000+ interactive components to mix freely

🔄 Iterative Design

① Use blueprint mode to build the "frame" first — wall positions and room sizes
② Walk in and feel the proportions — too big feels empty, too small feels cramped
③ Undo and redo in advanced building — until the proportions feel comfortable
④ Add furniture and decoration last — details are the final step
Don't finalize in one pass — good design is iterated.

Companion System

🐱🐕 Pet System · Complete Guide

The pet system has a strict unlock order: cat first, then dog — the basic cat-care tutorial is a hard prerequisite for dogs. Skipping or ignoring the cat system = you can never keep a dog.

📋 Cat vs Dog

Dimension 🐱 Cat 🐕 Dog

Prerequisite Just reach DG Lv.12 ⚠️ Must finish the basic cat-care tutorial
Daily interaction Petting, feeding, playing (cat toys) Petting, feeding, walking, playing (dog toys)
Interaction complexity ⭐⭐ Simple — cats are fairly independent ⭐⭐⭐⭐ More complex — needs walking and more attention

🔓 Unlock Flow: Cat → Dog (irreversible)

Step 1: DG Lv.12 → find Mrs Joan

After reaching DG Lv.12, go to the pet adoption center (suburbs) to find Mrs Joan. She'll guide you to unlock the cat hobby and let you adopt your first kitten. This is the gateway to the pet system.

Step 2: finish the basic cat-care tutorial ⬅ key!

After adopting your cat, Mrs Joan gives you the basic cat-care tutorial. It usually covers: feeding the cat → petting interaction → playing with cat toys → cleaning the litter box. The tutorial isn't long, but you must complete it all — it's the hard prerequisite for dogs. Skip it = never get a dog.

Step 3: daily cat care (during DG 12-14)

While waiting for DG Lv.14, interact with your cat daily to build affection. The cat's affection level won't reset when you later get a dog — the two pets' progress is fully independent. Consider raising the cat to at least affection level 2-3 before unlocking dogs.

Step 4: DG Lv.14 → unlock dogs!

With the prerequisite met (basic cat care finished), find Mrs Joan again at DG Lv.14 to unlock the dog hobby. After adopting your puppy, your pet system is complete — one cat and one dog, double the joy. Each pet has its own interactions, with affection rising independently.

💝 Daily Pet Care In Depth

🍖 Feeding

• Cats and dogs each have their own pet food (not interchangeable)
• Feed 1-2 times a day — no need to overfeed
• Pet food can be bought at the pet shop
• Higher-star pet food → more affection gain

🤝 Interaction

• Cat: petting, cat teaser, yarn ball
• Dog: petting, fetch ball, frisbee
• Each interaction can be done 1-2 times a day
• After interacting, pets show a mood icon (😊/😐/😿)

🚶 Walking (dog only)

• Dogs need regular walks — a dog-exclusive interaction
• Leashes are bought at the pet shop
• You can combine walk routes with your daily gathering route (two birds, one stone)
• While walking, you can see other players' dogs

📈 Affection System

• +1~3 affection per interaction (random, affected by pet mood)
• Higher affection → new interactions → home-score bonus
• Cat and dog affection are fully independent, tracked separately
• If you skip a day of interaction, affection does not decrease

❓ Pet System FAQ

🐱 Can I keep a dog without first keeping a cat?▾

No. The basic cat-care tutorial is a hard prerequisite for dogs. You must unlock cats at DG Lv.12 → adopt a cat → finish the care tutorial → then unlock dogs at DG Lv.14. This order is set by the game and cannot be skipped.

🐕 Can cats and dogs be kept together?▾

Yes! After unlocking dogs, a cat and a dog can live together in your home. They each have independent interactions and affection bars that don't affect each other. Consider interacting with the cat first (simpler) then the dog (needs walking, more time).

⏰ What happens if I forget to feed a pet one day?▾

A pet's affection won't drop if you miss a meal. If you forget to feed or interact one day, there's no penalty — you just don't gain new affection that day. This makes the pet system friendly to light players who don't log in daily.

⭐ What do you get at max pet affection?▾

At max affection, both cats and dogs contribute an extra home-score bonus. You also unlock all interaction actions and cosmetic options (like pet clothes and special toys). The pet system is a major part of pushing your home score to the top in the late game.

💤 Do pets affect the Dream system?▾

No. The pet system and Dream (DG Lv.16) are two completely separate systems that don't affect each other. You can keep pets and level Dream at the same time.
