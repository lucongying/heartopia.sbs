01Getting Started

Your First Three Days · Build a Money Loop

The beginner phase has one goal: reach DG Lv.5 and pick the Cooking hobby to build a daily loop of "farming → fishing → cooking → selling". Follow these three steps.

🎯

The core idea: push to DG Lv.5 as fast as possible → pick Cooking first → build a daily loop. Before that, don't waste Wishing Stars, don't fuss over decorating, and store every fish worth ≤100G for cooking.

📅 Day 1 · Explore the Island 🎣 Unlock Fishing

Explore + Villager Tasks

Tour the town center, suburbs, and fishing village, and mark Meadow Lake (daily-respawn Fluorite Mine & Tree Spirit Root). Complete villager tasks to reach DG Lv.2; Vanya will visit and give you a fishing rod.

💡 Zero Wishing Star spending · store fish worth ≤100G
📅 Day 2 · Start Farming 🌱 Unlock Gardening

Plant potatoes and save your first gold

Find Blanc to unlock Gardening and plant potatoes (3× the profit of tomatoes). Plant blueberries before bed and harvest in the morning; hit each fruit tree only once.

💡 Potato 35G cost → Fish & Chips worth 310G
📅 Day 3 · Cooking Takes Off 🍳 Unlock Cooking

Batch-cook with four stoves

At DG Lv.5 you must pick Cooking (Massimo · café). Batch-produce Blueberry Jam and Fish & Chips to push daily income past 5,000G+.

💡 4 blueberries (64G) → Blueberry Jam 170G, profit ×3-5

04Money Guide

Cooking Profit ×2-7 · From Newbie to Late Game

Remember three golden rules, then watch the "top recipe net-profit chart" below to bank 8,000-15,000G a day.

💰

Three golden rules: ❶ fish > 100G sell directly, ≤100G cook them   ❷ fruit into jam, vegetables into dishes, never sell raw ingredients   ❸ wheat is your economic lifeline — plant it whenever you can.

🏆 Top recipe net-profit chart (base price − ingredient cost)

⭐ Best Newbie Start

Blueberry Jam (64→170, net +106) + Fried Carp (80→350, net +250). Easy materials, low level requirement.

📈 Highest ROI

Vegetable Stew 456% · Fish & Chips 313% · Cheesecake 264%, your daily batch go-tos.

💵 Daily Tasks

5 villager tasks ≈ 7,000G/day + Wishing Stars + Contribution Medals — never skip them.

⚠️ Avoid These Pitfalls

Strawberries/grapes sell better raw — making jam is barely profitable. High-star fish (>100G) should be sold directly, not cooked.

06Event Calendar

Limited-Time & Daily Events

Never miss a limited reward: daily sea-fishing times, seasonal event re-runs and gacha pull strategy.

📅

Daily must-dos: Sea Fishing Tournament at 12:00 & 21:00 — attend both  ·  5 villager commissions ≈ 7,000G/day  ·  Exhibition Tickets carry over across pools — save them for what you want.

🕐 Daily Activity Timeline

Schedule by time for maximum reward

🌅 After Waking Up

Harvest blueberries, water crops, and check the pet shop's daily breeds (3 cats + 3 dogs).

📋 All Day

Complete the 5 villager commissions (the main DG XP source + Wishing Stars).

🎣 12:00

First Sea Fishing Tournament · arrive at the beach within 10 minutes to join.

🎣 21:00

Second Sea Fishing Tournament · the second wave of the day, don't miss it.

🫧 Every Saturday

15 Dream Bubbles refresh · find Bailey Jr. and play the white piano to trigger them, hiding exclusive furniture.

❄️ Snow Season · Re-run

Unlocks the Penguin Stove, the only source of iced coffee / iced latte. Frosting is capped at 50 per day — always buy the full amount.

🌸 Flower Festival · Spring

Best time for hybrid breeding — a friend's watering is required. Chrysanthemum path: red + white → pink → peach → green → Lunar 5★.

🎟️ Exhibition Gacha

Tickets carry over across pools — save them for the item you want; duplicates can be exchanged for specific items.

🏆 Fishing Tournament

Compete for biggest/rarest; rare fish spawn more often during the event. Stock up on bait and showcase high-star fish to climb the ranks.
