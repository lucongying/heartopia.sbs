About This Site

An unofficial guide site for Heartopia, built by players for players.

Who We Are

Heartopia.sbs is an unofficial guide site created and maintained by dedicated players of Heartopia. We are not the official team, nor are we affiliated in any way with the developers or publisher of the game — we are simply a group of fellow fans who love this game and want to organize the pitfalls we have hit and the experience we have gathered to share with more players.

What We Do

This site is dedicated to consolidating scattered information into a clear, searchable, up-to-date English resource library, covering the complete journey from your first day on the island to max-level graduation:

  • Beginner Guides and Progression Routes: the time-gated DG 1→20 progression order and daily must-do checklists
  • Eight Collectible Databases: fish, insects, birds, recipes, crops, NPCs, achievements, and sheet music
  • Interactive World Map: 300+ markers, with real-time search and route planning
  • Money-Making Guides and Redeem Codes: profit tables, fishing strategies, and the latest working redeem codes
  • 50+ Common Problem Troubleshooting: solutions for technical issues such as white screens, getting stuck, and multiplayer problems

Our Principles

We insist that content comes from real player experience and community research, cite sources wherever possible, and continuously follow game updates to correct data. If you spot errors or outdated content, please let us know via our Contact Us page and we will update it as soon as possible.
