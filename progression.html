🏅 Unlock Order & Decision Logic

Order DG Hobby Why Choose It What if you pick differently?
1 Lv.2 🎣 Fishing The Lv.2 route hobby — there is no alternative to weigh. Gives you a steady supply of fish, the base of most cooking recipes. —— (no alternative at this level)
2 Lv.3 🌱 Gardening The Lv.3 route hobby — again no alternative. Crops are the other half of the ingredient base: fish + crops = the recipe system. —— (no alternative at this level)
3 Lv.5 🍳 Cooking ⬅ Top pick! The one hobby that multiplies everything else you produce. Jams run roughly 2.4-2.7× the value of their ingredients and most other dishes about 1.3-1.5× — but star rating stacks on top of that, and a 5★ dish can be worth several times its 1★ version. That combination is why cooking is the standard first pick.
Bug catching and birdwatching are not dead ends — insect and bird cards both sell to Albert Jr. — they simply don't multiply anything.		 Picking bug catching or birdwatching first is not fatal, but it delays the cooking multiplier until Lv.8 — and gold is tightest in exactly that window, when you are still paying for seeds, expansions and tools.
4 Lv.8 🦋 Bug Catching Reasons: ① the inflatable bee lure (unlocked at Bug Catching Lv.4) draws a burst of insects to one spot for about 60 seconds with a chance at rare spawns, so you can farm without roaming; ② 5★ insect specimens feed your Home Evaluation score; ③ the bug shop stocks the Puzzle Dream you will want after Lv.16. Birdwatching is a perfectly good alternative here — the third ticket at Lv.11 lets you pick up whichever one you skipped.
5 Lv.11 🔭 Birdwatching Birdwatching's quirk is that photographing the same bird over and over still adds progress — you don't have to hunt down new species, so you can camp one spot and level in peace. Rarer poses score higher stars. Once done, all five production/collection hobbies are unlocked. —— (third ticket; only birdwatching is left to pick)
6 Lv.12 🐱 Cat Keeping The gateway to the pet system, and the hard prerequisite for dogs — Mrs Joan will not unlock dog keeping until cat keeping is done. —— (costs a hobby expansion ticket, so keep one spare)
7 Lv.12 🐕 Dog Keeping Unlocks at the same DG level as cats, once cat keeping is done and you have a second ticket. Dogs add walking and beanbag fetch on top of the shared feeding and petting loop. —— (requires cat keeping first, plus a second ticket)

📈 Leveling Tips for Each Hobby

🎣 Fishing Leveling

• Spots and weather are paired — sunny days favour river spots such as Glow River, rain favours shallow river and lake spots
• Timed sea-fishing events run daily at 12:00 and 21:00; the Sea Fishing Party is run by Bill at the fishing village pier
• Higher fishing level → higher chance of high-star fish → higher sale price

🌱 Gardening Leveling

• Rotate fast crops (potatoes: 30G seed, 1 hour) → frequent harvests, frequent XP
• Never leave a plot empty — replant as soon as you harvest
• High-grade flowers (rose, lily) score far better than basic flowers in Home Evaluation
• Gardening level raises the star rating of what you harvest

🍳 Cooking Leveling

• The common layout is three pots at 90° so you can reach them all without walking; the food-cart stove holds more (around 5-6)
• Batch-cook the same dish for maximum throughput
• High-value recipes to aim for: strawberry jam, blueberry jam, fish & chips
• Cooking level = higher star rate ↑ = higher price ↑

🦋 Bug Catching Leveling

• The inflatable bee lure unlocks at Bug Catching Lv.4 — drop it and nearby insect spawns spike for about 60 seconds, with a chance at rare species
• Different seasons and weather bring different insects
• 5★ specimens count toward Home Evaluation
• The bug shop sells the Puzzle Dream (for leveling Dream after Lv.16)

🔭 Birdwatching Leveling

• Photographing the same bird repeatedly still adds progress — no need to chase new species
• Find a fixed bird spot and level in peace
• Rarer poses and actions score higher stars
• Bird cards can be handed in to Bailey J, and sold to Albert Jr. for gold

🐱🐕 Pet Leveling

• Daily interaction (feeding, petting, teasing/training) raises a pet's growth value and level
• Cat keeping must be unlocked before Mrs Joan will unlock dogs
• Cats and dogs coexist — up to 5 cats and 3 dogs
• Reaching new pet levels pays out gold and Wishing Stars

Space Management

🎒 Backpack & Warehouse Expansion · Complete Guide

Backpack space is the most underrated resource in the game — not enough slots means frequent trips home while gathering, which halves your effective gathering time. Expansion has both a free track and a Wishing Star track; mixing them well maximizes value.

🆓 Free Expansion

The game automatically gifts warehouse expansions at specific DG levels — <strong>completely free, no NPC required</strong>:

DG Lv.2
📦 Warehouse +50 (→200)
DG Lv.7
📦 Warehouse +50 (→250)
DG Lv.13
📦 Warehouse +50 (→300)
DG Lv.17
📦 Warehouse +50 (→350)
DG Lv.19
📦 Warehouse +50 (→400)
📊 Total free expansion: 5 × 50 = +250 warehouse slots, and none of it needs any action — just reach the level. On free progression alone the warehouse runs 200 → 250 → 300 → 350 → 400 slots. Your backpack also grows with DG level on its own track (roughly 20 at Lv.1, 28 at Lv.5, 36 at Lv.10, 44 at Lv.15, 52 at Lv.20) before any purchases.

⭐ Wishing Star Expansion (Paid)

Find NPC Ka Ching (northwest suburbs, on Residential Street across from plot 8) to buy expansion tickets with Wishing Stars. Note: backpack and warehouse tickets are bought separately, and their discount counts are tracked independently.

Purchase Count Backpack Ticket Warehouse Ticket Price (Wishing Stars) Value
Times 1-5 +10 slots each +50 slots each 10 stars (60% off) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Best
Times 6+ +10 slots each +50 slots each 25 stars (flat — no further tiers) ⭐⭐⭐ Standard
🎯 Optimal strategy: the first 5 backpack tickets and the first 5 warehouse tickets are 60% off at 10 stars each. Buying both out = 100 Wishing Stars → +50 backpack slots and +250 warehouse slots. From the 6th purchase the price returns to the standard 25 stars and stays there — it does not keep climbing, so there is no deadline, just a discount worth taking first.
💰 Wishing Star budget: stars come mainly from the 5 daily resident requests (5 stars each, so up to 25/day), plus redeem codes, events and community rewards. Early on (DG 1-7) put them into the discounted expansions → first 5 backpack (50 stars) + first 5 warehouse (50 stars) = 100 stars → after that, buy further tickets at 25 stars whenever you actually need the space.

⚡ Growth Lv.5-12 Buy out the first 5 backpack + first 5 warehouse tickets (100 stars total); free +50 warehouse lands at Lv.7 Backpack 28 → 36 base, up to ~86 with all 5 tickets / warehouse 250

📍 Ka Ching's location: northwest suburbs, on Residential Street across from plot 8, in her general store. This NPC handles both backpack and warehouse expansion tickets. Note that they are two separate purchases — you buy each separately, and their discount counts are tracked separately. Buying both to 5 discounted tickets (50 stars each) before any other spending is the standard advice.
Homestead System

🏠 House Building System · In Depth

Heartopia gives you a basic placement mode from the very start, plus an advanced "god-view" build mode that you switch on yourself. The advanced mode is not a DG-level unlock — it is a toggle in Settings → Gameplay (in single-player you can also tap the button in the top-right corner; it cannot be switched on in multiplayer). Blueprints open around DG Lv.8.

🔷 Basic Placement vs Advanced (God-View) Building

Feature 🔷 Basic placement 🔶 Advanced (god view)
Availability Available from the start Toggle in Settings → Gameplay (single-player only)
Placing walls One block per placement ✅ Batch placement — drag the white arrow on a piece to copy a whole row
Laying floors One tile at a time, slow ✅ Batch operations — multi-select to build, paint or demolish a large area at once
View control Standard third-person view ✅ Top-down god view — pan, rotate and zoom, and hide floors to see inside
Furniture placement One by one, manual adjustment ✅ Multi-select — move or stow every piece on the same level together
Use cases Placing basic functional furniture (bed, cooking pot, storage box) Full decorating, Home Evaluation runs, large builds

🏗️ Early game: build for function, not for looks

Before you have a steady income and a stocked warehouse, don't sink hours into decorating. Placing pieces one at a time is slow, and the same wall is a single drag once you switch on advanced build mode.

Early on, only do this:
① Place functional furniture: bed, cooking pots, storage box, workbench
② Keep the interior open — leave room for a future design
③ Sketch floor plans (on paper or annotated screenshots) before you commit materials
④ Gather inspiration: visit friends' homes and screenshot layouts you like

Core principle: early gold and time belong in production (seeds, cooking pots, expansions), not in pure decoration.

🎨 Turn on advanced build mode and decorate

Switch advanced building on in Settings → Gameplay and the whole job gets dramatically faster. Suggested order:

Decorating flow:
① Floor plan first: decide each area's function (kitchen, bedroom, collection display, pet corner)
② Walls and floors: batch-lay floors → batch-build walls to separate areas
③ Roofline: stack and overlap roof pieces at different heights so the house isn't one flat box — but watch the load-bearing limit
④ Furniture layout: place by function, using multi-select to move whole groups at once
⑤ Finishing touches: wall art, ornaments, plants, lighting — these details define quality

Core principle: the build UI has an undo button — experiment with layouts and roll back what you don't like. Visit friends often and screenshot ideas.

💡 Advanced House Building Tips

🏗️ Open vs Compartmented

Open plan (few or no interior walls): strong sense of space, great for large collections, open sightlines. Good for beginners — fewer walls means fewer mistakes.
Compartmented (many rooms): clear function zones, more privacy, great for themed design (kitchen, study, sun room). Batch wall building makes this far less tedious.

📐 Roof Design Tips

① Stack and overlap: there is no roof "merge" function — you break up the box look by stacking and overlapping roof pieces at different heights
② Mind the load-bearing limit: cramming in structure can actually cost you Home Evaluation points
③ Colour matching: coordinate roof and wall colours — a dark roof over light walls is a reliable combination

⭐ Home Evaluation, and how to score well

Home Evaluation unlocks around DG Lv.12. You submit once a week (deadline Saturday, results early Saturday morning) and your best score is kept, so you can redecorate and resubmit.
• Variety is the core of the score — the first of an item is worth around 100 points, duplicates only about 1
• Furniture counts per category; premium and event furniture score higher
• 5★ collectibles (insect specimens, fish) and cooked dishes count
• Flowers score by grade and variety — swapping basic flowers for high-grade ones is a large jump
• Note: pets do not feed into your Home Evaluation score

🔄 Iterative Design

① Rough in the frame first — wall positions and room sizes
② Walk around inside and feel the proportions — too big reads as empty, too small as cramped
③ Undo and rebuild until the proportions feel comfortable
④ Add furniture and decoration last — details are the final step
Don't try to finalise in one pass — good layouts get iterated.

Companion System

🐱🐕 Pet System · Complete Guide

The pet system has a fixed order: cat first, then dog. Both unlock at DG Lv.12, but Mrs Joan will not unlock dog keeping until cat keeping is done — and each one costs a hobby expansion ticket.

📋 Cat vs Dog

Dimension 🐱 Cat 🐕 Dog
Unlock DG level Lv.12 Lv.12
Unlock NPC Mrs Joan (Pet Home · central plaza) Mrs Joan (same as above)
Prerequisite Reach DG Lv.12 + one hobby expansion ticket ⚠️ Cat keeping unlocked first, plus a second ticket
Daily interaction Feeding, petting, teasing (cat teaser), training moves Feeding, petting, walking, beanbag fetch, training moves (shake, spin)
Interaction complexity Simpler — no walking to fit into your day More involved — walking, plus cleaning up after them
Progress metric Growth value → pet level; new levels pay out gold and Wishing Stars Same growth/level system, tracked separately per pet

Can they coexist? ✅ Yes! Cats and dogs live together — up to 8 pets in total, each with its own progress

🔓 Unlock Flow: Cat → Dog

Step 1: DG Lv.12 → find Mrs Joan

At DG Lv.12, head to the Pet Home on the central plaza — a two-storey building (Bailey J's birdwatching study is upstairs). Hand Mrs Joan a hobby expansion ticket to unlock cat keeping. The adoption line-up refreshes daily and offers three breeds at a time.

Step 2: adopt a cat and finish "Learn a New Hobby" ⬅ key!

After adopting, Mrs Joan walks you through the basics: feeding → petting → teasing/play. Finishing this quest is what lets her unlock dog keeping later, so don't leave it half-done. (Cats have no litter box in this game — cleaning up after your pet is a dog-only chore.)

Step 3: keep a second hobby ticket in hand

Dog keeping costs another hobby expansion ticket, so don't spend your spare one elsewhere around Lv.11-12. There is no level to wait for — as soon as cat keeping is done and you have a ticket, you can go straight back to Mrs Joan.

Step 4: unlock dog keeping (still DG Lv.12)

Hand over the second ticket and dog keeping opens. From there you can build up to 5 cats and 3 dogs, each with its own growth progress. Dogs add walking, beanbag fetch (from Dog Keeping Lv.3) and picking up droppings, which become fertiliser.

💝 Daily Pet Care In Depth

🍖 Feeding

• Cat food and dog food are separate items — cat food from the cat-care store (around 150G), dog food from the pet store
• Satiety drains over time (roughly 200 minutes to empty), so most players feed 2-3 times a day
• Favourite and high-value foods (moonfish, sunfish) give extra growth — sunfish can trigger a "super full" state

🤝 Interaction

• Cat: petting, feeding, cat teaser / play (12 different poses), training moves
• Dog: petting, feeding, walking, beanbag fetch (from Dog Keeping Lv.3), training moves such as shake and spin
• Frequencies differ: petting can be repeated (about 3 times per 6 hours), play roughly once a day, bathing once or twice a week
• Yarn balls are a reward you get from petting, not an interaction of their own
• Personality matters — a skittish pet is more likely to break off an interaction

🚶 Walking (dog only)

• Walking is dog-exclusive, and it is how you collect "Dog Moments"
• Your dog will need to relieve itself — pick it up and it becomes fertiliser
• There is a special interaction in the Spirit Oak Pine Forest
• Walk routes combine well with your daily gathering loop

📈 Growth & Progress

• Petting, feeding and play raise a pet's growth value; reaching new pet levels pays out gold and Wishing Stars
• Higher levels unlock more interaction moves
• Each pet tracks progress independently — cats and dogs don't share
• Miss a day and you lose nothing already earned; only satiety and mood drop

❓ Pet System FAQ

🐱 Can I keep a dog without first keeping a cat?▾

No. Cat keeping is a hard prerequisite. Unlock cats at DG Lv.12 → adopt one → finish the "Learn a New Hobby" quest → hand Mrs Joan a second hobby expansion ticket, and dog keeping opens at the same DG level. There is no Lv.14 gate.

🐕 Can cats and dogs be kept together?▾

Yes! Once both hobbies are unlocked you can keep up to 5 cats and 3 dogs together — 8 pets in total. Each animal has its own growth progress and they don't affect one another. Cat slots open at cat-keeping Lv.1/2/5/7/9, dog slots at Lv.1/4/8.

⏰ What happens if I forget to feed a pet one day?▾

You don't lose progress. Growth value and pet level never go backwards, so a missed day just means no gain that day. What does drop is satiety and mood — a hungry, unhappy pet is more likely to refuse an interaction, so top it back up when you return.

⭐ What do pet levels get you?▾

Reaching new pet levels pays out gold and Wishing Stars and unlocks more interaction moves. There is also a "Cat Moments / Dog Moments" photo collection (1-5★) whose star totals unlock gold, Wishing Stars and furniture recipes. Pet outfits do exist (a headpiece slot and a body slot), but they come from the pet fashion store and Mrs Joan's shop — they are not a max-level reward. Note that pets do not add to your Home Evaluation score.

💤 Do pets affect the Dream system?▾

No. The pet system and Dream (DG Lv.16) are two completely separate systems that don't affect each other. You can keep pets and level Dream at the same time.
