🎵 Score Guide

How to Collect & Play Every Score

Everything worth knowing about sheet music: unlock, sources, difficulty tiers, and the rarest scores on the island.

🎵 Three rules to remember: ❶ unlock the Music hobby before you can learn any score   ❷ the 30 scores come from six sources — OST, main story, exploration, seasons, events & friendship   ❸ seasonal and event-exclusive scores are missable, so grab them before they rotate out

🎵 Unlock the Music hobby

Music is one of the hobbies you can unlock on the island. Once unlocked, your first piece — the Heartopia Main Theme — is granted automatically, so you can start playing right away.

📖 Six sources of sheet music

The 30 scores come from six groups: the game OST, main-story quests, area exploration, seasonal & festival events, NPC friendship, and hidden exploration. Cross-check each source to complete the collection.

🎯 Reading the difficulty tiers

Difficulty runs 1–5. ★1–★2 pieces are easy starters, while ★4–★5 pieces like the Legendary Ballad or Rainbow Bridge demand precise rhythm and high skill.

Where to find scores

🎬 OST & main story

The Heartopia Main Theme auto-unlocks with Music. Main-story quests grant Adventure March (DG Lv.5), Dream Departure (DG Lv.10) and Harvest Festival (DG Lv.15) as you progress.

🗺️ Area exploration

Each region hides a score once you arrive — Fishing Village, Forest, Hot Spring, Flower Field and Beach each hold an area-specific piece for your repertoire.

🍂 Seasonal & event-exclusive

Spring, summer, autumn and winter each offer seasonal pieces, plus festival and night-exploration scores. These rotate out, so collect them while they're available.

Difficulty · style · mood

⭐ Difficulty

Ratings span ★1 (easy, e.g. Fishing Village Ditty) to ★5 (Legendary Ballad, Rainbow Bridge). Higher difficulty means faster BPM, longer duration, and tougher rhythm.

🎼 Style

Scores span Classical, Folk, Jazz, Pop, March and World music, played across Piano, Violin, Flute, Guitar, Harp, Trumpet, Saxophone, Percussion and full Ensemble.

🎭 Mood

Each piece carries a mood — Warm, Cheerful, Serene, Passionate or Melancholy — and a BPM from 52 to 144. Match the vibe to the moment you play it.

🍂 Missable scores: don't let them rotate out

Seasonal and event-exclusive scores are missable — spring, summer, autumn and winter pieces plus festival scores only appear during their window. Cross-check the event calendar and grab them before they rotate out.

🚀 Quick tips

🌧️ Play in the rain & special spots

After getting a score, playing it in the rain or at special locations earns progress — the Music Beginner achievement is unlocked this way.

👥 Perform where NPCs gather

Playing in crowded areas earns more attention and racks up public performances fast — 20 public shows unlock the Street Performer achievement.

🎯 Master the rhythm for ★5 pieces

High-difficulty scores like the Legendary Ballad demand a flawless run — practice the rhythm repeatedly to nail a no-mistake performance (Perfect Performance, Music Lv.8).
