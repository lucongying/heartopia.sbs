🍳 Cooking Guide

How to Cook Like a Pro

Everything worth knowing before you fire up a stove: unlock, cooking levels, the batch method, and the recipes that make the most gold per ingredient.

🍳 Three rules to remember: ❶ Cooking unlocks at DG Lv.5 via Massimo   ❷ the 94 recipes split across Processing / Cooking / Dessert / Drinks / Events   ❸ process cheap ingredients instead of selling them raw

🍳 Unlock & your first stove

At DG Lv.5, spend your first hobby expansion ticket on Cooking with mentor Massimo (at the café). He also sells meat, cheese, cream and butter — plus recipe books as you level up.

📈 Cooking level 1–13

Level up by cooking dishes; most recipes are level-gated. Black Truffle Pie unlocks at Lv.10 via a trivia quest, and Steamed Golden King Crab is a Lv.10 recipe variant.

🔥 The four-stove method

Place 4 stoves in a square and stand in the middle to run 4 dishes at once — 4× output with overlapping cook times. This is the core of every profitable batch.

📈 Best recipes by stage

🌱 Early game (Lv.1–3)

Blueberry Jam (170G) and Fish & Chips (310G) are your first money-makers; Grilled Mushrooms (180G) and Fruit Platter (100G) round out the starter set.

🍳 Mid game (Lv.4–8)

Coffee (290G), Fish Curry (260G), Seafood Pizza (780G) and Cheesecake (480G) carry you through the middle levels.

💎 Late game (Lv.9–13)

Black Truffle Cream Pasta (900G), Steamed King Crab (1,990G) and the top-tier combo meals — Candlelight Dinner (1,760G) — are the endgame earners.

🥫 The profit rule: process, don't sell raw

Never sell cheap ingredients raw. 4 blueberries (64G) become Blueberry Jam (170–340G), and any cheap fish + potato becomes Fish & Chips (310–620G) — a 2–5× profit on the same ingredients. Save high-value fish (>100G) to sell directly and cook everything cheaper. The one exception: a King Crab is worth far more steamed (1,990G) than sold raw (535G).

🚀 Quick tips

🫐 Forage blueberries for jam

Blueberries are a forage item and can't be planted — pick them from bushes as you explore during the day and turn them into jam (170G+) for a zero-cost profit loop.

🔥 Batch on four stoves

Run all 4 stoves on one product — blueberry jam, fish & chips, or strawberry jam — to multiply output without extra effort.

📅 Don't miss event recipes

Whale-season dishes (Ocean Feast, 1,600G), Sanrio collab crepes (gacha) and the hidden Daisy / Rose teas are missable — grab them while their events are live.
