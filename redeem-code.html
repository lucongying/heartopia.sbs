⚠️ How this page works: every code below is cross-checked against an official or reputable source and carries a source link and a verification date. General/VIP codes that can't be traced to a specific official announcement are flagged as "community general codes" — confirm them on Discord before relying on them. How to Redeem

How to Use Redeem Codes

⌚

Step 1

Open the game and tap the watch icon in the top-right corner to open the menu

⚙️

Step 2

Go to Settings → tap the “Redeem Code” button in the bottom-right

📬

Step 3

Type or paste a code → confirm → claim your rewards from the in-game mailbox
