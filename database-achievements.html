🏆 Achievement Guide

How to Complete All 70 Achievements Efficiently

A completion route for the whole list — how achievements unlock, which rewards matter, and a plan for the 12 hardest ★5 goals.

🏆 Three rules to remember: ❶ achievements unlock as you level hobbies, not all at once   ❷ Wishing Stars and titles are the real rewards   ❸ the 12 ★5 achievements are long-term goals — start their grind early

🔓 How they unlock

Most achievements unlock automatically as you play — reach a DG level, unlock a hobby, or hit a milestone. The ★5 goals only appear once the matching hobby or feature is available.

⭐ What you earn

Every achievement rewards Wishing Stars (for backpack expansions) and many grant a permanent title. The ★5 ones grant the rarest titles like Fish King, Gourmet and Magnate.

🎯 Difficulty tiers

Ratings run ★1 (trivial) to ★5 (long grind). ★1–★3 come naturally; ★4–★5 need a focused plan — the 12 ★5 achievements below are the real time sinks.

🗺️ A sensible completion route

Work in this order: ① unlock every hobby first (fishing → gardening → cooking → bug catching → birdwatching)   ② clear the ★1–★3 achievements passively as you level each hobby   ③ then focus the ★5 goals, since they overlap — the "catch every species" grinds double as progress toward Ultimate Collector.

🚀 Quick tips

🏅 Stack the collection goals

Fishing, bug catching and birdwatching all feed Ultimate Collector — so chase rare species with the ★5 collection achievements in mind, not one at a time.

💰 Reinvest Wishing Stars

Prioritize fishing, gardening and cooking achievements first — they reward Wishing Stars you can spend on backpack expansions, which make every later grind easier.

📅 Watch the calendar

Several achievements (festivals, events, season-locked species) can only be done at the right time — check the calendar and season windows before you commit to a grind.
