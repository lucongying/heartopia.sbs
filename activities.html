Seasonal Events

Limited-Time Event Guide

Heartopia runs limited-time seasons and collaborations on a rolling schedule, each with its own exclusive items, recipes and furniture. Re-runs are not guaranteed — the events below have all closed on the global server

📌 Currently running: the Dave the Diver collaboration (8 Aug – 7 Sep 2026, global). See the archive table below for the full 2026 schedule.
Ended · 24 Jan – 14 Mar 2026

❄️ Winter Frost Season (Fashionwave season)

A winter limited-time event that unlocks the Penguin Stove — an essential for making iced drinks like iced coffee and iced latte. A normal stove can't make any iced drink.

Prerequisites

DG Level 7 and complete the main quest "The Answer Lies Within". Answer Atara's call and finish "File Speedrun" to start the preparation phase.

Day 1 → 🎣 Vanya

Complete the Night Fishing Coffee Pot quest. Begin preparing the event.

Day 2 → 🧪 Naniwa

Submit the Omniscient Booster. Keep advancing the preparation progress.

Day 3 → 💰 Mrs Joan

Pay 1,000 Gold. Complete the final round of preparation.

Day 4 → 📝 Opinion Survey

Open the resident opinion survey phase — the event fully kicks off.

⚠️ Key reminder: stock up on the seasonal ingredient Frosting daily — Massimo's shop caps most base ingredients at 50 per day, so buy the full amount every day of the event. White Radish Seeds are the season's exclusive seed at the gardening shop. You can also use the shared stove at Massimo's café as a Penguin Stove.

Strategy Tips

Event Participation Tips

📅

Plan the Calendar Ahead

Some seasons open with a multi-day preparation phase gated to one NPC task per day (Winter Frost worked this way) — each day's task must be done that day, so a missed day slips the whole schedule. Watch the official Discord announcements before a season goes live.

💰

Save Up Event Currency

Spend Moonlight Crystals and event-exclusive currency on limited banners first to pull exclusive outfits, furniture and vehicles. Don't blow them on the standard pool — miss an exclusive and you may wait a year.

🤝

Team Up to Double Efficiency

Flower breeding requires a friend to water before it triggers. During events, find partners on Discord or the world channel and help each other complete quest goals that need social interaction.
