01Getting Started

Your First Three Days · Build a Money Loop

The beginner phase has one goal: reach DG Lv.5 and pick the Cooking hobby to build a daily loop of "farming → fishing → cooking → selling". Follow these three steps.

🎯

The core idea: push to DG Lv.5 as fast as possible → pick Cooking first → build a daily loop. Before that, don't waste Wishing Stars, don't fuss over decorating, and store every fish worth ≤125G for cooking.

📅 Day 1 · Explore the Island 🎣 Unlock Fishing

Explore + Villager Tasks

Tour the town center, suburbs, and fishing village, and mark Meadow Lake (daily-respawn Fluorite Mine & Tree Spirit Root). Complete villager tasks to reach DG Lv.2; Vanya will visit and give you a fishing rod.

💡 Zero Wishing Star spending · store fish worth ≤125G
📅 Day 2 · Start Farming 🌱 Unlock Gardening

Plant potatoes and save your first gold

Find Blanc to unlock Gardening and plant potatoes (3× the profit of tomatoes). Forage blueberries for jam as you explore; hit each fruit tree only once.

💡 Potato 30G cost → Fish & Chips worth 310G
📅 Day 3 · Cooking Takes Off 🍳 Unlock Cooking

Batch-cook with four stoves

At DG Lv.5 you must pick Cooking (Massimo · café). Batch-produce Blueberry Jam and Fish & Chips to push daily income past 5,000G+.

💡 4 blueberries (64G) → Blueberry Jam 170G, profit ×3-5

04Money Guide

Cooking Profit · From Newbie to Late Game

Remember three golden rules, then cook at quality and sell the right things — the recipes and rules below are the version that holds up.

💰

Three rules that hold up: ❶ every priced crop sells for about 3× its seed (the outliers: pineapple 3.5×, carrot 3.1×) — so pick by gold/hour   ❷ ★★ = ×1.5 and ★★★ = ×2 the base price on all 73 recipes   ❸ avoid the value-destroyers: Grape Jam, Chocolate Sauce and Tuna Sashimi sell for less than their raw materials.

🏆 Top dishes by ★★ sell price (no profit figure — a third of ingredients have no price)

Cook at quality — ★★ is ×1.5 and ★★★ is ×2 the base price, and the biggest seller by far is Steamed Golden King Crab.

⭐ Best Newbie Start

Blueberry Jam (4 blueberries → 170) and Fish & Chips (2 fish + 2 potatoes → 310) are the honest newbie money-makers: forage-plus-a-fish, no special level, no crop seed.

📈 Highest ROI

Almost every priced crop sells for about 3× its seed. Pick by gold per hour: Tomato 80, Potato 60, Wheat 47.5 — not by looking at the sale price. (Pineapple runs 3.5×, carrot 3.1×.)

💵 Daily Tasks

Cook at star quality and sell the right things. ★★ is ×1.5 and ★★★ is ×2 the base price on all 73 recipes — no exceptions, so quality is free money.

⚠️ Avoid These Pitfalls

Three recipes destroy value: Grape Jam (4 grapes 1,920 → 220), Chocolate Sauce (1,320 → 240) and Tuna Sashimi (1,700 → 320). Cook them never.

📋 All Day

Complete the 5 villager commissions (the main DG XP source + Wishing Stars).

🎣 12:00

First Sea Fishing Tournament · arrive at the beach within 10 minutes to join.

🎣 21:00

Second Sea Fishing Tournament · the second wave of the day, don't miss it.

🫧 Every Saturday

15 Dream Bubbles refresh · find Bailey Jr. and play the white piano to trigger them, hiding exclusive furniture.

Ran 24 Jan – 14 Mar 2026. Unlocked the Penguin Stove, the only source of iced coffee / iced latte.
