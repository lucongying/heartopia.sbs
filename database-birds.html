🔭 Birdwatching Guide

How to Spot Every Bird on the Island

Everything worth knowing before you raise your binoculars: unlock, leveling, habitats, weather, and the rarest species to chase.

🔭 Three rules to remember: ❶ Birdwatching unlocks at DG Lv.11 via Bailey J   ❷ your Birdwatch level gates which species appear   ❸ photographing the same bird repeatedly still builds progress

🔭 Unlock & your gear

At DG Lv.11, spend your third hobby expansion ticket to unlock Birdwatching from mentor Bailey J — you get a camera and binoculars to start photographing birds.

📈 Birdwatch level 1–9

Level up by photographing birds. Rare species are level-gated — Imperial Shag, White-Headed Duck, American Flamingo and Paradise Tanager all need Lv.9.

📸 Shoot the same bird again

Unlike other hobbies, you don't need to chase new species — photographing the same bird repeatedly still builds your level, so camp a reliable spot.

Where to find birds

🌲 Forest & suburbs

Spirit Oak Pine Forest (Redpoll, Golden Bowerbird), the Deer Tower (Kestrel, Red Falcon) and the Suburbs (Zebra Finch, Eastern Bluebird, Paradise Tanager) are rich birding grounds.

🌊 Waterside & coast

Whale Sea (Audouin's Gull), East Sea / Zephyr Sea (Imperial Shag), Old Sea coast (Brown Noddy), Violet Beach (White Wagtail) and Forest Lake (Smew) cover the waterfowl and seabirds.

⛰️ Mountains & flower fields

Hot Spring Mountain (Eurasian Eagle Owl, Great Tit, Hawfinch) and the Flower Field (Eurasian Chaffinch, Orange-Bellied Fruit Dove, American Flamingo) hold many unique species.

Weather · time · rarity

🌦️ Weather

Most birds appear in any weather, but the rarest are pickier — the American Flamingo and Tern need rainbow weather, while the Eastern Bluebird and Peregrine Falcon favor rain or rainbow days.

🌙 Time

Almost every bird is active all day; only the Eurasian Eagle Owl is strictly nocturnal. No dawn-only birds to worry about.

🎯 Rarity & level gates

Rarity ★1 birds need just Lv.1–2, while ★4–★5 raptors and waterfowl are locked behind Lv.7–9. Level up before chasing the rarest species.

🚀 Quick tips

🔭 Level up before chasing rares

★4–★5 birds only appear at Birdwatch Lv.7–9, so grind levels on common birds first — the rarest targets become visible once you're leveled.

🌈 Save rainbow weather for rare spawns

A few birds (American Flamingo, Tern) only spawn in rainbow weather. When a rainbow hits, prioritize those over common birds.

📸 Equip your camera, not a net

Birdwatching uses only your camera and binoculars — no net or bait needed. Walk the map with binoculars equipped and snap every bird you pass.
