Seasonal Events

Limited-Time Event Guide

Heartopia regularly launches season-limited events with exclusive items, recipes and furniture — miss one and you usually wait a year for a re-run

Active / Re-run

❄️ Snow Season · Frost Event (Winter Frost Event)

A winter limited-time event that unlocks the Penguin Stove — an essential for making iced drinks like iced coffee and iced latte. A normal stove can't make any iced drink.

Prerequisites

DG Level 7 and complete the main quest "The Answer Lies Within". Answer Atara's call and finish "File Speedrun" to start the preparation phase.

Day 1 → 🎣 Vanya

Complete the Night Fishing Coffee Pot quest. Begin preparing the event.

Day 2 → 🧪 Naniwa

Submit the Omniscient Booster. Keep advancing the preparation progress.

Day 3 → 💰 Mrs Joan

Pay 1,000 Gold. Complete the final round of preparation.

Day 4 → 📝 Opinion Survey

Open the resident opinion survey phase — the event fully kicks off.

⚠️ Key reminder: the limited ingredient Frosting is capped at 50 per day — always buy the full amount! For White Radish Seeds, buy the "Trend Season exclusive seeds" at the gardening shop. You can also use the shared stove at Massimo's café as a Penguin Stove.
Coming Soon

🌸 Flower Festival · Blossom Carnival

A spring limited-time flower event and the best time to collect rare flowers and complete hybrid breeds. During the event, flowers grow faster and breeding success rises.

💡 Prep tips: reserve 2×2 or checkerboard planting spots in advance and stock up on Rainbow Powder. During the event, have friends water your flowers to trigger breeding — never harvest the parent flowers; keep them as infinite hybrid-seed generators. Chrysanthemum progression: red + white → pink (2★) → peach (3★) → green (4★) → Lunar (5★).
Held Regularly

🏆 Fishing Tournament

A competitive fishing event where players compete to catch the biggest and rarest fish. During the tournament, specific rare fish spawn more often; participating earns rewards, and higher ranks bring richer prizes.

Prep tips: repair your fishing rod in advance and stock up on bait. Different waters, weather and times yield different species — watch the fish shadow size (S/M/L) to pick your target. Showcase high-star fish to climb the ranks, and save low-star fish for cooking.

Strategy Tips

Event Participation Tips

📅

Plan the Calendar Ahead

Seasonal events usually have a 3-4 day preparation period; each day's task must be done that day — miss one and the whole schedule slips. Follow the official Discord announcements before an event goes live.

💰

Save Up Event Currency

Spend Moonlight Crystals and event-exclusive currency on limited banners first to pull exclusive outfits, furniture and vehicles. Don't blow them on the standard pool — miss an exclusive and you may wait a year.

🤝

Team Up to Double Efficiency

Flower breeding requires a friend to water before it triggers. During events, find partners on Discord or the world channel and help each other complete quest goals that need social interaction.
