🦋 Bug-Catching Guide

How to Catch Bugs Like a Pro

Everything worth knowing before you swing a net: unlock, tools, hot spots, weather windows, and the rarest insects on the island.

🦋 Three rules to remember: ❶ bug-catching unlocks at DG Lv.8 via Naniwa   ❷ the 67 species split across Forest / Flower Field / Hot Spring Mountain   ❸ most rare insects only appear in specific weather — time your hunts

🦋 Unlock & your first net

Pick Bug-catching with a hobby expansion ticket (DG Lv.8) and the mentor Naniwa — at the town center — sets you up with a bug net. It's a collection hobby, not a money-maker, so unlock Cooking first.

🛠️ Nets & inflatable traps

Buy bug nets and inflatable insect traps from Naniwa's shop. Traps passively lure exclusives like the Sulkowsky's Morpho, while you actively net the rest.

🎯 Approach slowly & read the weather

Equip your net and creep up on insects — moving too fast spooks them. Check the weather first: many butterflies spawn only on clear days, and rainbow weather unlocks several exclusives.

Where to hunt

🌸 Flower Field

The richest bug biome — butterflies (Peacock, Purple Emperor, Mother of Pearl), bees and flower beetles all cluster here. Best on clear days.

🌲 Forest & Forest Island

Night hunters should head here — the Hercules Beetle (400G), stag beetles and moths appear on tree trunks after dark. Forest Island adds the Alpine Longhorn Beetle in rainbow weather.

♨️ Hot Spring Mountain & elsewhere

Mantises, jewel scarabs and dragonflies dot Hot Spring Mountain, the Fishing Village, Suburbs and your Homestead — sweep these spots to fill the encyclopedia.

Weather · season · time

🌦️ Weather

Rainbow weather unlocks several exclusives — the Rainbow Stag Beetle, Crimson Marsh Glider, Alpine Longhorn Beetle and Cerulean Carpenter appear only then. Clear days favor butterflies; rain brings out dragonflies.

🍂 Season

Insects are spring, summer and autumn only — none spawn in winter. Summer is the busiest season, with most beetles and butterflies active.

🌙 Time

Butterflies and bees fly by day; moths, fireflies and the Hercules Beetle come out at night. Dusk and dawn catch the crepuscular species like the Mourning Cloak.

🎁 Bait events & trap exclusives

Six insects are bait-event exclusive — Apollo, White Witch, Rainbow Stag Beetle, Pink Katydid, Chestnut Tiger and Postman Butterfly — and won't spawn in the wild. The Sulkowsky's Morpho is trap-only, so keep an inflatable trap running.

🚀 Quick tips

🌈 Chase rainbow weather

Rainbow days unlock the Rainbow Stag Beetle, Crimson Marsh Glider and other rainbow-only insects — drop everything and hunt when one rolls around.

🌙 Hunt the forest at night

The most valuable bugs — Hercules Beetle (400G), Death's-head Hawkmoth (150G) — only appear on forest tree trunks after dark.

🎁 Naniwa loves the rarest bugs

Gift the Blue Morpho, Hercules Beetle, Death's-head Hawkmoth or Large Red Damselfly to Naniwa to raise friendship fast — but hold one for the encyclopedia first.
