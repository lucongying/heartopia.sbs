Money Guide

Covering the five core money systems: cooking profit, fishing strategy, daily quests, and Wishing Stars

Core principle

The golden rule of making money

💰 Three iron rules: ❶ sell fish above 100 gold directly, cook the rest ≤100   ❷ turn fruit into jam and vegetables into dishes — never sell raw ingredients   ❸ wheat is your economic lifeline; plant it whenever you can

🔬 Why does processing beat selling raw?

4 blueberries sold raw = 64 gold
Made into blueberry jam = 170-320 gold
Profit multiplies 2.7-5x

🐟 The fish dividing line: 100 gold

Sardine 40G → cook into fish and chips for 310G
Tuna 300G → sell directly; cooking loses money
Remember the key threshold: 100G

🌾 Wheat = the economic engine

Truffle pasta, seafood pizza, fish and chips
Almost every high-end recipe consumes wheat
Plant before bed; a crop every 4 hours

Daily routine

The 10-minute efficient money loop

A standardized routine to run after each login, with an expected yield of 8,000-15,000 gold per round

① Morning: harvest + plant (~2 min)

Harvest potatoes/wheat/corn and replant. Prioritize potatoes (3x the yield of tomatoes) and wheat (core ingredient in all late-game high-profit recipes). Plant blueberries and strawberries whenever you have seeds.

② Gather: free ingredient sweep (~2 min)

Run the map collecting berries, mushrooms, fruit, and herbs. In autumn, focus on black truffles (200G each, 3-4x profit after cooking). Free = zero cost = 100% pure profit.

③ Fish: filter for high value (~3 min)

Head to the ocean for high-value fish (tuna 300+, pufferfish 450). Keep low-value fish ≤100G (sardine, crucian carp, sea bass) as cooking material. Remember the threshold: sell anything above 100 gold, cook anything below.

④ Cook: four-stove batch production (~3 min)

Arrange 4 stoves in a square and stand in the middle to manage 4 dishes at once. Prioritize batch production: jam (fruit → 170G+) + fish and chips (fish + potato → 310G) + vegetable medley (3 vegetables → 200G).

⑤ Sell + villager quests (~2 min)

Find the wandering merchant Albert Jr. and batch-sell all finished goods. On the way, complete 5 villager quests — each rewards an extra 1,000-3,000 gold + Wishing Stars + contribution medals.

🏆 Highest net profit

Truffle cream pasta (+630), steamed king crab (+445), truffle mushroom pie (+395) — but ingredients are rare, so make them whenever you have the materials.

📈 Highest ROI

Vegetable medley (456%), fish and chips (313%), cheesecake (264%), fried crucian carp (250%) — your top picks for daily batch production.

⭐ Best beginner start

Blueberry jam (64→170, a reliable 106) + fried crucian carp (80→350, a reliable 250). Both use easy-to-get ingredients and require a low Cooking level.

⚠️ Pitfall warning

Strawberries and grapes are better sold raw (30-40G each); jamming them leaves razor-thin profit. Save high-value fruit for direct sale, and only make jam from low-value fruit (blueberries at 16G).

Fishing for money

Fishing strategy · from novice to fishing king

Fish is the most abundant free resource in the game — the key is knowing what to keep and what to sell

🎯 The 100G threshold rule

≤100G → keep for cooking: sardine (40), crayfish (45), goldfish (50), sea bass (55), common carp (65), crucian carp (80)

>100G → sell raw: catfish (110), salmon (120), trout (250), eel (280), tuna (300), pufferfish (450)

Legendary: legendary koi (2000) and rainbow fish (5000) — sell them! Cooking is a pure loss.

📍 High-profit fishing spots

🏆 Ocean (summer): tuna 300G + pufferfish 450G → 500-1000G per trip
🥈 Ocean (winter): king crab 600G → rare but very profitable
🥉 Starlight Sea (spring · rainbow weather): rainbow fish 5000G → one catch lifts you out of poverty
⭐ Secret pond (night): legendary koi 2000G → a lucky gamble
🌊 River (all year): crucian carp 80G → make fried carp for steady profit

📅 Seasonal fishing calendar

🌸 Spring: trout 250G (mountain lake · daytime), rainbow fish 5000G (Starlight Sea · rainbow weather)
☀️ Summer: tuna 300G (ocean), pufferfish 450G (ocean · daytime), crayfish 45G (river → make sashimi)
🍂 Autumn: eel 280G (river · rainy night), salmon 120G (river / fishing village)
❄️ Winter: king crab 600G (ocean) → use full ingredients for steamed king crab sold at 1100G

💡 Tips to maximize quest efficiency

Stockpile common items early

Keep in storage: 5-10 each of potatoes, wheat, blueberry jam, fish and chips, and vegetable medley. Hand them in the moment a quest arrives.

Take quests before heading out

Check what the 5 quests are each login, then head out and complete them in one trip — do not go back and forth.

Grow multiple crop types

Do not plant just one crop. Keep a few plots each of potatoes, tomatoes, carrots, and corn so you always have what a quest asks for.

Cooking quests = stacked profit

If a quest needs a cooked dish, make extra: hand in 1 for the quest + sell 4 more = quest reward + sale profit.

Wishing Star economy

⭐ The smartest way to spend Wishing Stars

Wishing Stars are the scarcest currency in the game — spending them in the wrong place is costly

🏅 Wishing Star spending priority (high to low)

🥇 Top priority: bag & storage expansion (first 5)

The first 5 bag (+10) and storage (+50) expansions cost only 10 Wishing Stars each (normally 25). It is the best investment in the game — bag space directly affects production efficiency. From the 6th purchase it jumps to 25 stars and becomes far less worth it.
Advice: buy out the first 5 as soon as possible = 50 stars for +50 bag slots +250 storage slots.

🥈 Second priority: hobby unlocks & upgrades

The three money-making hobbies — Fishing (Lv.2), Gardening (Lv.3), and Cooking (Lv.5) — must be unlocked first. Leave bug-catching (Lv.5/8/11) and pets (Lv.12/14) for later — they do not directly produce gold.
The higher your Cooking level, the better your odds of high-quality dishes — the same ingredients earn 50-100% more.

🥉 Third priority: buildings & decorations (functional)

Extra stoves (one more stove = 1.3x cooking efficiency), more fields (higher crop yield), and functional furniture. These directly boost your earning efficiency.
Decorative furniture is the lowest priority — pretty, but it produces no gold.

💡 Quick-cash trick: Wishing Star → firecracker → 1100G

Buy a firecracker with 1 Wishing Star → sell it to Albert Jr. = 1,100 gold. A quick way to cash out when you need money — but in the long run, Wishing Stars are far more precious than gold, so do not do this often.

⚠️ Never spend Wishing Stars on these

❌ Speeding up crop growth (waiting 2h is not worth 1 star)  ❌ Buying base ingredients (grow/fish/gather them free)  ❌ Pure decoration (only buy later with spare stars)

Advanced tips

Key strategies quick reference

🔥 The four-stove method

Arrange 4 stoves in a square and stand in the middle to manage 4 dishes at once. 4x output with overlapping cooking times for maximum efficiency.

🛒 First 5 expansions for only 10 stars

The first 5 purchases of bag +10 and storage +50 cost only 10 Wishing Stars (normally 25) — always buy them all. 50 stars total.

🌾 Plant wheat heavily, sow before bed

Almost every high-profit recipe consumes wheat. Plant wheat before bed and harvest 4 hours later when you wake. Grow it in bulk.

🚀 Trade Wishing Stars for firecrackers

Buy a firecracker with 1 Wishing Star → sell it for 1,100 gold. A quick cash-out for emergencies, but do not use it often.

🍳 Push Cooking level first

The higher your Cooking level, the better your odds of ★★/★★★ results — the same ingredients sell for double. Lv.12, which unlocks truffle cream pasta, is the key milestone.

🧠 Batch-production mindset

Do not cook piecemeal — stockpile a batch of ingredients and run 4 stoves at once. Also batch-sell to Albert Jr. to save travel time.

📦 Storage management rules

Sell high-value fish (>100G) the same day, and keep at most 10 low-value fish as backup. Stockpile jam and dishes and sell them in bulk once you have enough.

🔄 Crop rotation strategy

Grow wheat (4h) long-term, rotate potatoes (2h) during the day, and plant blueberries (6h) before bed. Ensure you always have crops growing.
