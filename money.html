Core principle

The golden rule of making money

💰 Three rules the data actually supports: ❶ every priced crop sells for about 3× its seed price — pineapple 15→52 (3.5×) and carrot 50→155 (3.1×) are the outliers — so growing alone has a fixed ceiling   ❷ cooking is only worth it when you hit ★★/★★★ — the multiplier is exactly 1.5× and 2× on all 73 recipes we have recorded   ❸ some recipes destroy value: never turn grapes into jam

🔬 Growing has a fixed ceiling

Almost every crop we have priced sells for about 3× its seed — potato 30 → 90, wheat 95 → 285, avocado 180 → 540 — with two exceptions: pineapple 15 → 52 (3.5×) and carrot 50 → 155 (3.1×).
So crop choice is only loosely about margin. The real variable is gold per hour.

⭐ The star multiplier is the real profit

★★ = 1.5× and ★★★ = 2× the base price, with zero exceptions across 73 recipes.
Strawberry Jam: 1,580 at ★, 3,160 at ★★★. The ingredients cost the same either way.

🌾 Wheat is in more recipes than anything else

Wheat appears in 25 of our 73 recipes — pizza, pies, pasta, cakes, bagels and risotto all consume it.
4 hours to grow, 95 a seed, 285 a harvest. Keep a plot on it permanently.

Daily routine

A login loop that follows the numbers

Five steps in the order that matters. We deliberately do not quote a gold-per-hour figure — that depends on your farm size, hobby levels and luck, and any number we printed would be made up.

① Harvest and immediately replant

Empty ground earns nothing, so replant before you do anything else. If you are farming purely for gold, short crops win: tomato 80/h, pineapple 74/h, tea leaf 66.7/h, potato 60/h. If you are farming to cook, plant wheat regardless of its 47.5/h — 25 recipes need it.

② Sweep the map for foraged ingredients

Mushrooms, berries and truffles cost nothing but walking time, which makes anything built from them pure profit. Grilled Mushrooms takes 4 mushrooms → 180 (360 at ★★★), and Blueberry Jam takes 4 blueberries → 170.

③ Fish, and sort the catch as you go

58 of the 83 fish we have priced sell for under 125G, so most of what you pull up is cooking stock. The expensive ones go straight to the seller untouched: tuna 850, golden king crab 750, catfish 610, king crab 535, eel 380.

④ Cook in batches, and chase the star rating

The same ingredients pay 1.5× at ★★ and 2× at ★★★, so cooking level is worth more than recipe choice. Reliable batches: Fish & Chips 310 (2 cheap fish + 2 potatoes), Rustic Ratatouille 640 (tomato + potato + lettuce), Tomato Sauce 180 from 4 tomatoes.

⑤ Sell everything in one trip

Albert Jr. is the game's only buying NPC, so there is no shop to price-shop against — the only thing that raises your take is processing and star rating. Save one trip, sell the whole stack, and pick up villager quests while you are there.

🏆 Biggest gain over selling the ingredients raw

Only 11 recipes have every single ingredient priced in our data. Among those, cooking adds the most here: Steamed Golden King Crab +730 (3 crabs worth 2,250 → 2,980), Steamed King Crab +385 (1,605 → 1,990), Rustic Ratatouille +85, Strawberry Jam +80.
At ★★★ the same four add +3,710 / +2,375 / +725 / +1,660 instead.

📈 Best return on the seed money

If you grew the crop yourself, the only gold you actually spent was the seed. On that basis: Pineapple Jam 367% (60 → 280), Tomato Sauce 350% (40 → 180), Rustic Ratatouille 246% (185 → 640), Strawberry Jam 216% (500 → 1,580).
Both cards are true at once — they just answer different questions.

🎁 The ones that cost nothing at all

Foraged ingredients have no purchase price, so the whole sale is profit: Mandarin Jam 270, Raspberry Jam 250, Grilled Mushrooms 180, Blueberry Jam 170, Mixed Jam 160, Fruit Platter 100.
All of them are available with no cooking level at all — this is the honest early-game income.

⚠️ Four recipes that destroy value

Grape Jam: 4 grapes sell for 1,920 raw, the jam for 220. Chocolate Sauce: 4 cacao beans 1,320 → 240. Exquisite Afternoon Tea: ingredients worth 2,640 → 950. Tuna Sashimi: 2 tuna are 1,700 raw → 320.
All four are still under water at ★★★. Sell those ingredients instead.

Fishing for money

Fishing: the weather is worth more than the spot

Our fish data records a location, a weather condition and a time window for every species. Most are "any weather, all day" — the ones that are not are where the money is.

🎯 Where the break-even sits

Under 125G → cooking stock. Sardine 50, carp 65, crucian carp 75, bass 75, sea bass 75, crayfish 100.

Over 125G → sell untouched. Salmon 150, burbot 200, pufferfish 230, goldfish 250, eel 380.

Two exceptions only: king crab and golden king crab, whose recipes take three of the same crab and still gain.

🌧️ Rain at night is the best window

Two of the highest-value freshwater fish share the exact same condition — rain, at night:
🐟 Catfish 610 · pond
🐍 Eel 380 · river

Nothing else inland comes close. If it starts raining after dark, drop what you are doing and go fishing.

🌈 Rainbow weather unlocks the rare ones

Some species only appear under a rainbow:
🦀 Golden King Crab 750 · sea fishing
🐟 Bluefin Tuna 300 · Zephyr Sea, day & dusk
🎏 Butterfly Koi 240 · Meadow Lake
🐟 Huchen, chum salmon · rivers, 40-50 each

Rainbow weather is also when the golden crab run is worth 2,980 a dish.

Daily loop

Where your daily gold actually comes from

This section used to carry a table of estimated daily incomes. Every figure in it was unsourced, so we removed them all rather than dress up guesses as data. What follows is only what we can point at a record for.

📋 Villager quests

Villagers post requests that pay gold on top of Wishing Stars and contribution medals. The medals are the part that matters most — they gate your Development Guild level, and DG level is what unlocks gardening tiers, free storage expansions and new hobbies.

Requests fall into a handful of shapes: deliver an item, catch a fish, forage something, cook a dish, or supply building materials. Because they can ask for almost anything, the cheapest way to clear them is breadth — a few plots of several crops rather than one field of the same thing.

We previously listed a quest count, a reset time and a gold payout per quest. None of them held up when we went looking for a source, so they are gone.

💡 What to keep in storage

Stock the cheap staples

Potatoes, wheat, tomatoes and a stack of Fish & Chips cover most delivery and cooking requests. All four are cheap to replace and none of them are worth hoarding for their own sale value.

Read the requests before you leave home

Check what is being asked for first, then plan one loop that covers all of it. The travel time is the expensive part, not the items.

Plant variety, not volume

Keep a few plots each of potato, tomato, carrot and corn. A request for two carrots you do not have costs a two-hour wait; a request for a crop you already grow costs nothing.

Cook the whole batch

If a request wants one cooked dish, make several while the stove is going. You hand in one and sell the rest, and the ingredients were already gathered.

Wishing Star economy

⭐ Where Wishing Stars are actually spent

Stars buy capacity, and capacity is what lets you batch. Almost everything else people claim about them turns out to be unsourced.

🏅 Spending priority

🥇 Buy the first five expansions of each, and stop to think after that

The first five bag expansions (+10 slots) and the first five storage expansions (+50 slots) cost 10 Wishing Stars each. From the sixth onward the price is 25 stars — and it stays at 25, it does not climb further.
100 stars gets you +50 bag and +250 storage. That is the single best rate in the game and it never comes back.

🥈 Do not buy what your DG level hands you free

Storage expands on its own at DG Lv.2, 7, 13, 17 and 19 — +50 slots each time, +250 in total, at no cost. The free ceiling is 400 slots.
Bag capacity also grows with DG on its own, from roughly 20 slots at Lv.1 to about 52 by Lv.20. Check what is coming before you spend.

🥉 Hobbies are not bought with stars

Fishing opens at DG Lv.2 and gardening at DG Lv.3. Beyond those, new hobbies come from Hobby Expansion Tickets awarded at DG Lv.5, 8 and 11 — three of them, for seven hobbies.
Spend the first ticket on cooking. It is the only hobby that multiplies the output of the other two.

⚠️ A popular trick that does not exist

You will find guides claiming that one Wishing Star buys a firecracker you can resell for 1,100 gold. We could not find any support for it. One star does buy a colourful skyrocket, but it is a one-shot consumable and there is no such resale price. This page carried that claim until we checked it; it is gone now.

Quick reference

Eight things the numbers tell you

⭐ Cooking level beats recipe choice

★★ pays 1.5× and ★★★ pays 2× the base price, on all 73 recipes with no exceptions. Moving a dish from ★ to ★★★ is worth more than switching to a fancier dish, and it costs no extra ingredients.

🌾 Keep one plot on wheat permanently

Wheat is in 25 of our 73 recipes — pizza, pies, pasta, cakes, bagels, risotto. Its 47.5 gold per hour is mid-table, but running out of it blocks more recipes than any other shortage.

🐟 The fish line is 125, not 100

Fish & Chips takes two fish and two potatoes for 310. Counting potatoes at their 30G seed price, cooking stops paying once a fish is worth more than 125G. Counting them at their 90G sale price, the line drops to 65G.

🦀 Bank king crabs in threes

Steamed King Crab needs three king crabs and nothing else: 1,605 of raw fish becomes 1,990, or 3,980 at ★★★. The golden version takes three golden king crabs, 2,250 raw, and sells for 2,980.

🚫 Never jam grapes or cacao beans

Four grapes sell for 1,920 raw and Grape Jam sells for 220. Four cacao beans are 1,320 and Chocolate Sauce is 240. Both stay deep in the red even at ★★★. Sell the fruit.

🍍 Pineapple is the best jam by return

Four pineapple seeds cost 60 and the jam sells for 280 — a 367% return on what you spent, the highest of anything we can fully price. Pineapples also grow in 30 minutes.

🌧️ Fish when it rains after dark

Catfish (610, pond) and eel (380, river) both require rain and night together. They are the two most valuable inland fish, and the window is narrow enough that most players never see it.

🔄 Match crop length to how long you are away

Tomatoes finish in 15 minutes and pay 80 an hour; avocados take 13 hours and pay 27.7. Short crops win only if you are there to replant. Sow the long ones before you log off.

Roadmap

What to make at each cooking level

Most recipes are gated behind a cooking level and bought from Massimo's shop. Here is the order they become worth your time. We have not put daily income targets on these — see the section above for why.

✅ Grilled Mushrooms 180 — foraged, free, no level needed
✅ Blueberry, raspberry and mandarin jam, 170-270 — also free
✅ Fish & Chips 310 at cooking Lv.1
✅ Plant tomatoes and potatoes and sell them raw

✅ Rustic Ratatouille 640 at Lv.3 — 185 of seed
✅ Smoked Fish Bagel 520 at Lv.4, one fish of any kind
✅ Cheesecake 480 at Lv.7
✅ Seafood Pizza 780 at Lv.8 — the best use of a 50G fish

✅ Steamed King Crab 1,990 at Lv.10
✅ Black Truffle Pie 830 at Lv.10, from a lore quest
