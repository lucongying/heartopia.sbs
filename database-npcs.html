👥 NPC Guide

How to Know Every NPC on the Island

Everything worth knowing about the 20 residents, mentors and merchants: unlock order, gifting, friendship, and which characters matter most for your progress.

👥 Three rules to remember: ❶ mentors unlock your hobbies in order — Vanya (Lv.2) → Blanc (Lv.3) → Massimo (Lv.5)   ❷ gift favorite items daily to raise friendship fast   ❸ buy Ka Ching's discounted backpack expansions first

🎓 Mentors unlock your hobbies

Five mentors gate the five production hobbies: Vanya (Fishing, DG Lv.2), Blanc (Gardening, Lv.3), Massimo (Cooking, Lv.5), Naniwa (Bug-catching, Lv.8) and Bailey J (Birdwatching, Lv.11). Meet them in order to unlock each system.

🎁 Favorite gifts = fast friendship

Every NPC has a list of loved items. Gift them daily to raise friendship, which unlocks personal stories and perks. Always check the Favorite Gifts column before spending rare items.

🛒 Merchants & services

Albert Jr. pays a premium for your goods (he roams the map), Ka Ching sells discounted backpack expansions, and Dorothy hands out daily food quests worth ~7,000G. Stop by every daily loop.

📍 Where to find them

🏙️ Town center

The busiest hub: Massimo (café), Naniwa, Bailey J, Annie, Atara, the Collector and Dorothy (clothing store) all work here, available all day. Most are within a short walk of the plaza.

🏡 Suburbs & garden street

Blanc (Garden Street), Ka Ching (west suburbs), Andrew (east side) and Bob (central shopping street) serve the farming and shopping side of the island — all available all day.

🏖️ Outlying areas

Residents spread across the map: Eric (Hot Spring Mountain), Will (Flower Field), Patti (Forest Island), Vernie (Violet Beach), Bill (Fishing Village) and Doris (Art Street). Albert Jr. roams everywhere with no fixed spot.

🎁 Gifting & friendship

❤️ Loved vs. liked gifts

Loved gifts give the most friendship. Cheapest high-value picks: Grilled Mushrooms, Blueberry Jam and Fish & Chips are loved by several NPCs at once — cook a batch and hand them out.

⏰ Gift every day

Friendship grows one gift at a time, so make gifting part of your daily loop. Mentors especially reward you over time with new recipes, tools and level-up perks.

🏆 Friendship rewards

Raising friendship unlocks personal stories and, for key NPCs, better prices or services. Check each NPC's description for what they offer at higher friendship.

Ka Ching's first 5 expansions cost only 10 Wishing Stars (originally 25) — always buy all of them for +50 bag slots and +250 storage before spending stars on anything else.

🚀 Quick tips

💐 Gift what they love

Check the Favorite Gifts column and gift loved items daily — friendship and its perks snowball. Grilled Mushrooms and Blueberry Jam are cheap and loved by many NPCs.

🗺️ Spot Albert Jr. on your route

He roams with no fixed spot and pays more than shops. Keep an eye out during your daily map laps and batch-sell everything at once.

📋 Check quests & restocks daily

Dorothy's clothing stock and daily food quests, plus Bob's general store, restock every day — visit them on every loop so you never miss a reward.
